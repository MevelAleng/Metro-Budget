# Metro Budget Overview

**Prepared by Meve Lako** | Power BI report for a Finance Department persona
**This is a class project, not affiliated with the Metropolitan Government of Nashville and Davidson County.

## What this is
An interactive report that looks at how well Metro Nashville planned its budgets from 2010 to 2024. It compares what Metro planned to spend (the budget) with what it actually spent.

## The questions
1. Can we trust Metro's budgets?
2. Is its planning getting better or worse over time?
3. Where do the misses come from?

## What's in the report
- **Title page: ** Start here button
- **Budget Accuracy Overview: ** Shows how closely actual spending matched the budget each year, highlighting overspending, underspending, and how they can cancel each other out to create a misleading picture of accuracy.
- **What Changed: ** Compares Metro’s most accurate years (2017–2019) with 2020–2024, highlighting which departments, spending categories, and budget variances increased over time.

## Key findings
- From 2010–2024, individual budget lines missed projections by $9.8 billion, while the net gap was only $1.9 billion, masking the true scale of budget inaccuracies.
-  Budget planning was strongest during 2017–2019.
-  After 2019, budget misses increased significantly, with larger annual dollar variances.

## How to use it
- Use the Year and Era filters to explore specific periods.
-  Navigate between pages using the buttons.
-  Right-click a year on the overview and select Drill through to view its detailed breakdown.
-  Use saved views to switch between department and fund breakdowns

## About the data
Public Data Source: Metro budget and actual spending data from 2010–2024, organized by fund, department, business unit, and account.

## Good to know
-  Absolute Difference: Measures the total size of all budget misses, whether over or under budget, without allowing them to cancel each other out.
-  Accounting adjustments were excluded because they do not represent actual spending.
-  The report identifies where budget variances increased but does not explain the underlying causes.
